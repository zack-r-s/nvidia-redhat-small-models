# Mark I local PyAT evaluations

These are the original pre-reconciliation case sets, traces, and reports. The measured artifacts were moved here without changing their contents. Their scores are historical and should be cited as Mark I.

The frozen `pyat-v001/cases.jsonl` and `pyat-v002/cases.jsonl` are the inputs for comparable Mark II reruns. New results belong under [`../mark-ii/`](../mark-ii/README.md).
